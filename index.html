      DAY 2

      How to survive

      • Find three things: the Basement Key, the Bolt Cutters and the Master Key. Then reach the front door.
      • Every action makes noise. Walking +10%, the creaky hallway +5% more, searching +25%, the hammer +50%.
      • At 100% noise it comes for you. You hide, and must answer 1–3 horror trivia questions before the timer runs out.
      • Survive and it walks away (noise drops to 20%). Fail and you wake up the next day in the bedroom.
      • Get caught on Day 5 and you never leave.
      • Hide under the bed to calm the house (−30% noise, once per visit).
      • The basement is dark. Use the flashlight (F) — but light spilling under doors makes walking a little louder.
