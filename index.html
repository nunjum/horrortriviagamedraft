    DAY 2

    How to survive

    • Escape the house. Find the Basement Key, the Bolt Cutters and the Master Key, then open the front door.
    • She hunts by sound. Walking is quiet, running is loud, sneaking (C) is silent. Doors, drawers and the hammer are loud. At 100% noise she knows exactly where you are.
    • Listen. Her footsteps come from where she really is — wear headphones. Muffled means there's a wall between you.
    • Hide in wardrobes, closets, the pantry, under the bed, behind the shower curtain or the boiler. If she saw you go in, she'll check — answer her horror trivia before the timer runs out to stay hidden.
    • If she grabs you, answer one question to tear free. Fail and you wake up the next day. Fail on Day 5 and you never leave.
    • Your flashlight (F) lets you see — and lets her see you from much further away.
    • She hums when she’s calm. When the humming stops, hide.
    • Not everything in the house is her. Others stand in the corners and doorways. They don’t chase. They just watch. Don’t walk up to them.
    • The trivia covers The Haunting of Hill House, Bly Manor, Midnight Mass, The Midnight Club, The Fall of the House of Usher and Flanagan’s films.
